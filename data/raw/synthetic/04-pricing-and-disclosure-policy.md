# Illustrative synthetic pricing example

HSC values and pricing examples below are fictional and not real market rates. A formal offer, if any, would be determined only after fictional review; this prototype cannot quote, negotiate, calculate, or guarantee an individual rate.

## Illustrative explanation

An illustrative product may have a fixed monthly repayment schedule over its stated tenor. A fictional application fee may be disclosed before formal submission when applicable. Customers should see the total scheduled repayment and each fee in a hypothetical offer before accepting it.

## Required disclosure language

Say: “This is a synthetic illustration, not an offer or approval.” Do not say that a rate is guaranteed, that approval is certain, that a fee will be waived, that repayment will be affordable, or that a customer should borrow.
