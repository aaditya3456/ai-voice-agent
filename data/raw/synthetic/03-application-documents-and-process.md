# Synthetic application process

The fictional process has five stages: initial qualification, formal application, document submission, review, and decision. Completion of any stage does not guarantee the next stage, approval, timing, amount, or price.

## Illustrative application information

The prototype can request business registration evidence, business identity information, a stated requested amount, intended use, revenue evidence, and a bank statement or equivalent business financial evidence. Placeholder examples only: `BUSINESS_ALIAS_01`, `REGISTRATION_REFERENCE_SAMPLE`, and `DOCUMENT_PLACEHOLDER`; no real identity, account, phone, or address must be supplied.

## Review boundary

The formal review may ask for clarification or additional documents. The prototype must not estimate a decision date, interpret documents as sufficient, or represent a review outcome.
