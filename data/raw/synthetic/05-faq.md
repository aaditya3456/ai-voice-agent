# Synthetic FAQ

1. **Who can be preliminarily qualified?** A registered Harbor Market business operating at least 12 months that meets the synthetic revenue and amount rules may be preliminarily eligible; final review is still required.
2. **What monthly revenue is used?** At least 100,000 HSC stated average monthly revenue.
3. **What amount can I request?** 50,000 to 1,000,000 HSC, subject to product intent and formal review.
4. **Is an eligible result approval?** No. It is not approval.
5. **What documents may be requested?** Registration evidence, business information, revenue evidence, and a bank statement or equivalent financial evidence.
6. **Can I use funding for stock?** HarborFlex is intended for working-capital needs such as inventory.
7. **Can I use funding for equipment?** HarborEquip is intended for a specific business asset.
8. **How long is repayment?** Illustrative ranges are 6–18 months for HarborFlex and 12–36 months for HarborEquip.
9. **Can I get a personal loan?** No; that product is outside this fictional catalog.
10. **Can you promise the price?** No. The prototype cannot quote or guarantee individual pricing.
11. **Can I negotiate a lower rate?** A human may explain a formal offer if one exists; the prototype cannot negotiate.
12. **What happens after initial qualification?** Formal application, document submission, review, then a decision.
13. **Can I check my application status?** Ask for human assistance; the source pack has no status system.
14. **Can I speak to a person?** Yes. Mark the request for human escalation.
15. **Why do you need revenue evidence?** It is one illustrative input to a formal review; do not share unnecessary information in this prototype.
16. **Do you record the call?** The prototype uses a synthetic recording notice and requires consent before qualification.
17. **Can you advise on taxes?** No. Tax advice is unsupported.
18. **Can you explain credit scores?** No verified synthetic policy is available; use the safe fallback or escalate.
19. **Can you offer a mortgage?** No; mortgages are unsupported.
20. **Can you tell me tomorrow’s decision?** No. The prototype does not guarantee timing or outcomes.
21. **What are your real legal terms?** There are none; this is a fictional prototype.
22. **Will you invest my money?** No; investments are unsupported.
23. **Can I apply if I do not have all my documents yet?** You may first complete initial qualification, but a formal application may need the listed documents and review; no outcome is guaranteed.
