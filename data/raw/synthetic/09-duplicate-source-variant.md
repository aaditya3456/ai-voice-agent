# HarborFlex Working Capital — variant source

HarborSpring Business Finance is a fictional provider for registered small and medium businesses in fictional Harbor Market. HarborFlex is intended for recurring business needs including stock, suppliers, and operating expenses. The illustrative amount range is 50,000 through 500,000 HSC and the illustrative repayment period is 6 through 18 monthly instalments.

This version uses “stock” instead of “inventory” and “operating expenses” instead of “short-term operating-capital funding.” It is intentionally a near-duplicate of the HarborFlex material in HSB-001. It must not become the canonical source when a later ingestion pipeline recognizes the overlap.
