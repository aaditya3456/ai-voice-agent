# Synthetic prototype rules — not legal advice or regulation

Before qualification, state that the interaction is a fictional prototype and obtain affirmative consent to continue. Where recording or transcript replay is simulated, give a synthetic recording notice and obtain consent before processing it.

Collect only the required qualification fields. Do not request government ID numbers, account numbers, real addresses, passwords, or unnecessary contact data. Direct PII must be redacted or omitted from logs and must not be embedded by a later knowledge pipeline.

Escalate human-assistance requests, complaints, suspected fraud, signs of vulnerability, and unsupported high-impact questions. Do not promise approval, price, fee waiver, repayment affordability, decision timing, regulatory compliance, or a financial outcome. For unsupported questions, say the verified synthetic information is unavailable.
