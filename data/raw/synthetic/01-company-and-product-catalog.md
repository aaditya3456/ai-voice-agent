# HarborSpring Business Finance

HarborSpring Business Finance is a fictional lender serving registered small and medium businesses in the fictional **Harbor Market**. HSC is a fictional unit used only in this prototype. HarborSpring supports working-capital and equipment-use applications; it does not offer personal loans, mortgages, investments, insurance, cryptocurrency services, or tax advice.

## HarborFlex Working Capital

For eligible registered businesses seeking inventory, supplier, staffing, or short-term operating-capital funding. Illustrative request range: 50,000 to 500,000 HSC. Illustrative repayment range: 6 to 18 monthly instalments. It is not a revolving credit facility and preliminary qualification is never an approval.

## HarborEquip Business Asset

For eligible registered businesses seeking equipment that supports their operations. Illustrative request range: 100,000 to 1,000,000 HSC. Illustrative repayment range: 12 to 36 monthly instalments. The formal application may request evidence of the intended asset and revenue evidence.

## Choosing a product

Working capital is intended for recurring business needs; HarborEquip is intended for a specific business asset. The prototype may explain those intended uses, but cannot recommend a product as suitable for a particular customer or promise terms.

## High-level process and servicing

The fictional process is initial qualification, formal application, document submission, review, then a decision. Existing fictional customers may ask a human support team for servicing assistance. Application status, final terms, or decisions are not available from this catalog.
